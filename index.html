01 / Research

Local rules.
Global behavior.

I use probabilistic, analytic, and combinatorial methods to understand emergent scales, limit laws, and phase transitions in large interacting systems.

  • Random growth & optimization

    First- and last-passage percolation; long-range and spread-out networks; random vertex environments; fluctuation limits and universality.

  • Network population dynamics

    Chemotaxis and aggregation on graphs and graphons; pattern formation; the effects of network structure on population abundance.

  • Evolutionary dynamics

    Evolutionary game theory and multilevel selection; scaling limits that connect individual- and group-level interactions to population dynamics.
