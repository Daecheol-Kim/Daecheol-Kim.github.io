Hi, I’m a Ph.D. student in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Illinois. I study probability theory and mathematical biology.

My advisor is Partha S. Dey. I also work closely with Daniel B. Cooney. I’m a member of the Eggs Lab.

My winding number around the Earth is 2.

dk43@illinois.edu

Research

My research interests include first- and last-passage percolation, random geometry, population dynamics on networks, evolutionary game theory, and multilevel selection.
